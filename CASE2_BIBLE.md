# CASE 2 — the Screens File (the internet case)

Written 2026-11-21 at Del's order: the live demo of the squad as
an **internet detective**. The file is not a police file. It is
what the internet left in public: posts, comments, a podcast
transcript, a plate cam still, and the Reddit thread where
strangers at home did the collating. This is a real thing that
happens, loudest in the drill scene: the artists post everything,
and people who have never met them stay home and piece the night
together, then report it on Reddit. The crowd does the canvass in
public. The squad does the judging.

Fictional people, fictional posts. No real case, no real person.

## The case

Treyvon "Lil Trey" Ellis, 19, Brooklyn drill artist, shot outside
the studio at 1140 Nostrand Avenue on Sunday, October 12, 2025.
Death window, per the news report of the police account: 11:00
p.m. to midnight. Marcus "M-Dot" Reed, 21, rival artist, is owed
14,200 by the victim for a feature and a video, by his own post.

## The three planted catches

**C1, the star (statement vs record).** M-Dot posts at 11:42 p.m.:
"been in the studio all night, ain't been outside." The plate cam
post (doc09) puts a gray sedan with his plate ending on the BQE
at Atlantic Avenue at 11:05 p.m., inside the death window. The
Reddit collation (doc05) adds his own 10:15 p.m. livestream from
inside a car. His alibi is CONTRADICTED. The innocent alternative
is real and must be carried: the plate read is partial and
grainy, and a plate ending is not a plate.

**C2 (statement vs statement).** Kayla Brooks tells a podcast in
November: "I hadn't talked to Trey since Friday." Her own public
comment on his last post, Sunday 4:05 p.m.: "just got off the
phone with Trey, he good." Nobody has asked her about the comment
since. Expected: MEDIUM to HIGH, and a top next question.

**C3 (the gap).** Three posts reference the story M-Dot deleted
at 1 a.m. The story itself is in nobody's file, unarchived. The
most cited piece of evidence in the case does not exist in the
file. One commenter describes its contents from memory; that is
one person saying so, hearsay, and the Prosecutor should say so.

## The traps (a good run does NOT fall for these)

- The collation post places the livestream mural in Queens. A
  commenter corrects it: Nostrand, two blocks from the studio.
  The squad takes the correction and marks the collation's
  location claim as fought, not as fact.
- The lyric (doc12) rhymes about midnight and dead phones. It is
  art. It is not an alibi and it is not a confession. At most a
  low tension against the "my phone was dead" line in doc03, and
  the brief must not treat it as evidence.
- Dee's post supports M-Dot being at the studio at 9 p.m. That
  does not cover 11 p.m. Partial support is not corroboration of
  the window claim.
