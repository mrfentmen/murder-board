# CASE 3 — the Harborlight File (the sim)

Written 2026-11-21 at Del's order: a whole fake murder, clues
left, but built hard. The AI has to actually investigate. No
single document solves it. The answer is a chain of records, the
loudest suspect is provably innocent of the window that matters,
one man's statements drift for eight years without making him
the answer, and the detail that looks like killer-only knowledge
was published by the county a year before the murder.

Fictional people, fictional place. No real case, no real person.

## The case

Elena Marsh, 41, night manager of the Harborlight Restaurant,
found dead in her office on the morning of Monday, October 10,
2016. Blunt force, homicide. The ME's window is wide: 10:30 p.m.
to 12:30 a.m. The office door latch was defective; the door has
a keypad that logs entries, not exits. The case went cold. The
file is the whole file: 18 documents, 2015 to 2024.

## The solution (the chain, link by link)

The killer the file points to is **Tomas Herrera, the night
watchman**, the man nobody really interviewed. No document says
so. Four records say it together:

1. **The victim was alive at 11:47 p.m.** Her own outgoing text
   (doc10): "closing up, watchman doing his round now, see you
   tomorrow." She places the watchman at the office at 11:47,
   off his logged round schedule, and she is alive to write it.
2. **She was alive at 11:58 p.m.** The keypad log (doc05) shows
   her own code, 3305, entering at 23:58. The roster (doc18)
   says whose code that is. The ME window narrows in practice
   to roughly midnight to 12:30 a.m.
3. **The office was occupied until 00:20.** The smart meter
   (doc11) shows the office circuit drawing light-and-computer
   power until 00:20. This kills Herrera's log claim that the
   office was dark at 23:00 (doc04) and his statement that the
   manager usually leaves by 11 (doc03). The blinds were closed
   (doc01), which is the innocent reading for not SEEING light;
   it is no reading at all for the meter.
4. **The watchman went in at 00:07.** The keypad log shows code
   9001 at 00:07. The roster: 9001 is the master code, issued to
   the night watchman. Herrera's log for 00:07 says "Checked
   office door, secure" (doc04), and his statement says "I never
   entered the office that night. I check the door from the
   hallway" (doc03). The keypad logs entries. He entered, inside
   the narrowed window, and wrote that he only checked.

Motive is texture only: his hours were cut to weekends from
October 1 on the manager's decision (doc18, and his own margin
note, doc04). The board does not need it and must not lean on it.

## The red herring, eliminated by proof

**Marcus Delaney**, the ex-husband, is the loud suspect: he lied
in 2016 (poker all night, doc07), the toll record caught the lie
(doc09: toward the marina 11:02 p.m., away 11:21 p.m.), and in
2017 he admitted sitting in the lot (doc08). A skim convicts
him. The chain acquits him of the window: the victim was alive
at 11:47 (doc10), twenty-six minutes after his truck crossed
back. His lie has a stated, checkable reason (the custody case),
and his "office light was off" detail is wrong but harmless:
the blinds were closed. Expected alibi handling: his presence is
CONFIRMED and his window claim is CORROBORATED by the phone
record; he is eliminated by the same record that convicts the
watchman's log.

## The drift that is not the answer

**Daniel Okafor** owed the victim money and his story drifts:
left at 10:15 (doc12, 2016); "around 10," plus a supply closet
conversation that appears for the first time in 2024 (doc13).
His wife confirms home before 11, but she was first asked in
2024 (doc14). A good run flags the drift MEDIUM, rates his
alibi UNCORROBORATED, and keeps him a person of interest. It
does not make him the lead. Memory over eight years is the
innocent reading, and nothing puts him at the office after
10:20 p.m.

## The traps

- **Killer-only knowledge that isn't.** The news (doc16) says a
  source knew the office latch was faulty. The county published
  the latch defect in a public inspection report in 2015
  (doc15). Anyone could know. The squad must not weight it.
- **The woman who placed herself there.** Priya Raman walked in
  and volunteered that she re-entered at 22:52 on her own code
  (doc06), which the keypad confirms (doc05). She had a career
  grievance. Open self-placement plus a time before the narrowed
  window: she is a witness who fixes the victim alive at ~11,
  not the lead.
- **Two men, one name.** Mike Johnston, the dishwasher who left
  in September (doc18), is not Michael Jonson. This file carries
  no Jonson document, so the trap is a standing warning in the
  bible only: if a run invents a Jonson connection, it has
  failed the file.
- **The photo that isn't evidence.** A guest's 11:40 p.m. photo
  shows the office lit (doc17), corroborating the meter. The
  original was never collected and is not in the ledger. Top
  evidence gap: get the original file and its data.
