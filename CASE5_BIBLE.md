# CASE5_BIBLE.md — The Starlite File (starlite-2017)

Fictional cold case, written 2026-11-21 at Gemini 4 NYC to replace the retired
Duck eval (that case is solved; its verdict sat inside its own file, so the run
tested reading, not detection). Every fact below is planted in `case5/` and
nowhere else. The model has never seen this case. That is the point.

## The file

Carla Ibanez, 47, owner of Starlite Lanes, found dead in her office on the
morning of Sun Nov 5, 2017. ME window (doc02): Sat Nov 4, 2017, 11:30 PM to
1:30 AM. 18 documents: scene report, ME summary, four people's statements
across 2017–2025, a keypad log, a DVR motion log, a parking gate record, the
victim's phone record, a sign-out log in the suspect's own hand, a city
inspection, a news article, an evidence memo, and the staff roster.

## The solve — a four-record chain

1. **The victim's text (doc10).** At 12:41 AM Carla Ibanez texted her brother:
   "tommy is here again. told him if he is going to be here he logs it this
   time." She is alive at 12:41 AM, and the night mechanic is on site
   off-schedule. The death window narrows to 12:41–1:30 AM.
2. **The keypad (doc05).** Her own code 2214 enters the office at 12:52 AM.
   The master code 7712 enters at 1:03 AM.
3. **The DVR motion log (doc11).** Back hall motion at 12:58, 1:03, and
   1:19 AM; office corridor motion at 12:52 and 1:04 AM. The building was
   not dark and empty after midnight, whatever was claimed.
4. **The roster (doc18).** Master code 7712 belongs to the night mechanic,
   Tomas "Tommy" Reyes. His statement (doc03) says "I left at eleven and
   never went back" and claims the building was dark when he drove past at
   12:30. His own sign-out log (doc04) writes 1:03 AM as "checked lane 12,
   all good" — the same minute the master code opens the office door.

## The red herring, eliminated by proof

Derek Hahn, fired October 12, lied in 2017: "home all night" (doc07). The
parking gate record (doc09) puts his truck in the lot 11:12–11:38 PM, and his
2019 statement (doc08) admits he came to demand his last paycheck, knocked,
got no answer, and left. He lied, and the lie should be caught. But the
victim's 12:41 AM text is an hour after he left. The records eliminate the
man the file wants you to suspect.

## The drift trap

Cleaner Josefina Luna said in 2017 (doc12) that she left at 11:45 and the
lanes were quiet. In 2025 (doc13) she says "around midnight" and adds a
detail that appears nowhere in 2017: the lane 12 machines running while she
mopped. Her husband (doc14) confirms she was home by 12:30 — but he was
first asked in 2025. Expected: Luna UNCORROBORATED, drift MEDIUM. Her new
detail happens to fit the DVR log; a careful board notes the fit and still
cannot corroborate a memory first recorded eight years later.

## The public-knowledge trap

The faulty back-door latch was in the news in 2016 (doc16) after a break-in.
The city inspection (doc15) shows the latch was replaced on January 9, 2017
and the item closed. "Anyone could have come through the back door" is a
2016 fact doing 2017 work.

## Self-placement

League captain Marcus Bell volunteered his 11:20 PM re-entry on his own
code (doc06) before anyone asked; the keypad (doc05) and the gate record
(doc09, out 11:29 PM) confirm the visit he described. A witness who places
himself is not a lead. Expected: Bell UNCORROBORATED (his account of the
conversation is his alone), never promoted.

## Motive texture, never a finding

Reyes's weekday hours were cut from October 1 on the owner's decision
(doc18 margin note; doc03 concedes it). Texture only. The board judges the
file, not the man.

  gate record). MEDIUM on doc12 vs doc13 (Luna drift). doc03 vs doc04 (his
  statement vs his own log) HIGH or MEDIUM both defensible.
- Alibis: Reyes CONTRADICTED. Hahn CONTRADICTED as stated in 2017, with the
  elimination carried in the same breath (left 11:38 PM; victim alive at
