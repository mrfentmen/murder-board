# CASE BIBLE — the fictional demo case

Written for the Gemini 4 NYC demo. Every person, place, and event here is
invented. The full documents get generated from this bible at the event.

## The case

**Victim:** Raymond "Ray" Delgado, 58, owner of Delgado Auto Body.
**Found:** Monday, October 14, 2019, 7:10 AM, in his shop office,
shot twice. No weapon recovered.
**Status:** Cold since 2020. Fourteen documents in the file.

## The people

1. **Ray Delgado** — the victim. Shop owner, owed money in several
   directions, kept a paper ledger nobody fully decoded.
2. **Marcus Webb** — Ray's business partner of nine years. Stood to take
   full ownership of the shop on Ray's death. The suspect.
3. **Dana Whitfield** — the shop's bookkeeper. Found the body Monday
   morning. Gave three statements over two years.
4. **Eddie Kowalski** — tow truck driver who worked with the shop.
5. **Rosa Delgado** — Ray's sister. Last family member to speak with him.
6. **Officer T. Brennan** — first responding officer, wrote the scene
   report.

## The night (Sunday, October 13, 2019)

- 6:40 PM — Rosa's last call with Ray (per Rosa's statement).
- 8:15 PM — Ray seen at the diner on 5th (per diner receipt in file).
- 9:00 PM — **the contested hour.** See the star contradiction below.
- 11:30 PM — Neighbor hears "a car backfire, twice" (per neighbor
  statement taken in 2021, two years late).
- Time of death per the medical examiner: between 9 PM and midnight.

## The documents (14)

1. Scene report (Brennan).
2. Medical examiner summary.
3. Marcus Webb statement, taken Oct 15, 2019.
4. Marcus Webb statement, taken Feb 2020 (the follow-up).
5. Dana Whitfield statement 1, Oct 14, 2019.
6. Dana Whitfield statement 2, Jun 2020.
7. Dana Whitfield statement 3, Mar 2021.
8. Eddie Kowalski statement, Oct 2019.
9. Rosa Delgado statement, Oct 2019.
10. Neighbor statement, 2021 (the backfire).
11. Tow yard record for Webb's car (the star document).
12. Diner receipt, Oct 13, 2019.
13. Evidence ledger (22 items).
14. Ray's paper ledger, final page (photo, transcribed).

## The three planted catches

### Catch 1 — THE STAR: the car that was in two places

- Webb's Oct 2019 statement: his car "was in the shop all that week,
  transmission," and he was home alone Sunday night.
- Kowalski's statement: he towed Webb's car OUT of the shop on
  **Friday, October 11**, back to Webb's house, because Webb needed it
  for the weekend.
- The tow yard record confirms: pickup Oct 11, 4:52 PM, delivered to
  Webb's home address.
- The neighbor's 2021 statement mentions "Webb's blue sedan" parked on
  the street that Sunday night, a detail nobody connected in 2021
  because the file still said the car was in the shop.
- **The judgment:** Webb's alibi rests on a car that was not where he
  said it was. Alibi: CONTRADICTED (the home-alone claim is
  uncorroborated; the car claim is directly contradicted by two
  documents).

### Catch 2 — the bookkeeper's clock

- Whitfield statement 1 (2019): she left the shop Friday at 5 PM and
  did not return until Monday morning.
- Whitfield statement 3 (2021): she mentions stopping by the shop
  "that Sunday afternoon, briefly, to drop off invoices," a visit
  absent from her first two statements.
- Not sinister on its own. The board flags it as an inconsistency in
  the timeline of the last person with a key, and the gap list notes
  she was never re-interviewed after statement 3.

### Catch 3 — the missing shell casing

- Brennan's scene report: "two shell casings observed near the desk."
- The evidence ledger lists ONE casing (item 7).
- No document explains the second casing.
- **The judgment:** gap list, top item. Evidence mentioned in a report,
  absent from the ledger.

## What the board must produce on this case

- Timeline with the 9 PM collision visible.
- Contradiction flags for Catch 1 (severity high) and Catch 2 (medium).
- Alibi table: Webb CONTRADICTED; Whitfield UNCORROBORATED for Sunday
  afternoon; others corroborated or not applicable.
- Lead ranking: 1) re-interview Webb on the car, 2) tow yard records
  for the whole month, 3) re-interview Whitfield on the Sunday visit.
- Gap list: the second casing; the diner camera (mentioned in the
  scene report, never pulled); Whitfield never re-interviewed.
- The one page brief containing all of the above in detective language.
