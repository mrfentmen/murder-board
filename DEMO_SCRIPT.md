# DEMO SCRIPT — 3 minutes, word for word

Del delivers. Beats are timed to the screen. The site is
murder-board.pages.dev. If the room runs slow, cut lines marked
(cut), never cut the starred lines.

## 0:00 — the file, and the order

* "This is a homicide file. Fourteen documents, six years cold." *
(The screen shows the case folder: CASE FILE MB-2019-1014, the
registry under it.)
"A partner found shot in his own auto shop in 2019. Statements,
records, a tow log, an evidence ledger. Everything a detective
would have. Nothing a detective has time to re-read."
(Tap the chip in the squad room: Work the case.)
* "I do not press a button. I give an order, in the room, and
the squad goes to work." *

## 0:25 — the room works

(The chat fills. The chairs light one by one. The board builds
behind it.)
"Ten chairs, four Gemini 4 models, every agent named. Voss logs
the file. Halden sets the death window. Ibarra puts the whole
case on one clock, on Hydrogen, the world model. Quill works
the machines on Neon. They post in the room as they finish,
and every report is their real output: counts, quotes, gaps."
(cut) "Nothing enters this board without the quote it came
from. A claim with no quote does not exist here."

## 1:10 — the catch

* "And then Vane cross-examines. Argon. The judge." *
(Vane's post is in the room. Click the red yarn on the board;

## 2:30 — the real one, the brief, the close

(Tap the chip: The Starlite case. The folder changes: MB-2017-1104,

"One more, and nobody has ever seen this one before today. A

## If a judge asks the hard questions

- "Is this real police data?" — "No, and that is deliberate.
  All four cases are fictional, written for this event, so the
  board can only read, never recall. We ran a real solved case
  this morning and retired it ourselves: the verdict was
  inside its own file, so the run proved reading, not
  detection. Real department files need chain of custody and
  local processing. That is the roadmap, and we say so."
- "Does it accuse people?" — "Never. It judges consistency of
  the file. Every flag carries an innocent alternative, and
  the detective weighs it."
- "Is the chat scripted?" — "No. Every line is composed from
  that step's actual output: the quotes, the counts, the
  scores are the run's own. The Runner's lines are the quote
  check dropping findings and the rubric correcting scores,
  in the open."
- "Is this a replay?" — "No. The squad makes live model calls for
  this run. Each room message is composed from that chair's actual
  output. The page does not load saved outputs or replay chat."
- "What exists already?" — "Truleo summarizes cases, Conectir
  checks statements. Nobody packages the full judgment, alibi
  ratings, lead scores, and the evidence ledger cross-check,
  aimed at homicide. That is the alley."
- "Why Gemini 4, and which models?" — "All four, four jobs.
  Argon judges: cross-examination and the lead. Carbon builds
  the book. Hydrogen holds the world: the Analyst's clock and
  map. Neon works the machines: cameras, keypads, plate
  readers. Chosen, not defaulted."
- "The criteria name a Decisions API." — "No decision model
  exists on this event's key; we checked the live list. The
  decision function here is the rubric recompute in the
  runner: deterministic, inspectable, arithmetic shown.
  EVAL.md states it in full."
