# DEMO SCRIPT — 3 minutes, word for word

Del delivers. Beats are timed to the screen. If the build runs slow,
cut lines marked (cut), never cut the starred lines.

## 0:00 — the file

* "This is a homicide file. Fourteen documents, six years cold." *
(Hold up nothing. The screen shows the document list.)
"A partner found shot in his own auto shop in 2019. Statements,
records, a tow log, an evidence ledger. Everything a detective
would have. Nothing a detective has time to re-read."
(Click Work the case.)

## 0:25 — the sort

"Four agents work the file. Intake reads every page and pulls out
every person, every claim, every event, every piece of evidence.
Nothing enters this board without the quote it came from."
(The board builds: person cards, evidence cards.)
"Timeline puts the whole case on one clock. There is the night of
October 13th, on a single line."

## 1:10 — the catch

* "And the Cross-Examiner reads the file against itself." *
(Click the red thread.)
"Marcus Webb, the partner, told detectives his car was in the shop
all that week. Transmission. He was home alone the night Ray
Delgado died."
(Second quote opens.)
* "The tow record, page eleven of the same file, says his car was
towed back to his house on Friday. Two days before the murder." *
"And a neighbor, interviewed two years later, saw that car on the
street that Sunday night. Three pages. Six years. Nobody ever put
them next to each other. The board did it in under a minute."

## 1:55 — the judgments

(Judgments screen.)
"It does not stop at sorting. It judges the file. Webb's alibi:
contradicted, and here is exactly why, in the file's own words."
(Alibi table.)
"The bookkeeper's story changed across three statements. Flagged.
Not accused. Flagged."
(Gap list.)
* "And the scene report says two shell casings by the desk. The
evidence ledger lists one. Six years, and nobody asked where the
second casing went. That is line one of the gap list." *

## 2:35 — the brief, and the close

(Generate the brief. One page.)
"One page. The case, the strongest contradiction, the alibi table,
the three leads worth tomorrow morning, and what was never done."
* "It does not solve the case. It is an AI investigator of death,
and this alley, the cold file nobody has time to re-read, is where
it works. The detective still does the detecting." *
(End. Do not add a thank-you line; let the brief sit on screen.)

## If a judge asks the hard questions

- "Is this real police data?" — "No. A fictional case written for
  this event. Real files need chain of custody and local processing.
  That is the roadmap, and we say so."
- "Does it accuse people?" — "Never. It judges consistency of the
  file. Every flag carries an innocent alternative, and the
  detective weighs it."
- "What exists already?" — "Truleo summarizes cases, Conectir
  checks statements. Nobody packages the full judgment, alibi
  ratings, lead scores, and the evidence ledger cross-check, aimed
  at homicide. That is the alley."
- "Why Gemini?" — "Four agents, four jobs. The reasoning model
  cross-examines, the fast model does intake. Chosen, not
  defaulted."
