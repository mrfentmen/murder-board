## Contradictions

**C1 — THE STAR (HIGH, record vs statement).** Webb's car.
- A (doc03, Webb, Oct 2019): "My car was in the shop all that week."
  Repeated in doc04 (Feb 2020): "Like I said in October, the car was
  at the shop."
- B (doc11, tow yard record, Oct 11 2019): pickup at Delgado Auto
  Body, "4:52 PM", vehicle "2014 Ford Taurus, blue sedan",
  "Drop-off location: Webb residence, 412 78th Street."
- Supported by doc08 (Kowalski): "I towed Mr. Webb's blue sedan out
  of the shop on Friday, October 11, and took it to his house. He
  said he needed it for the weekend."
- And doc10 (Vasquez, 2021): "There was a blue sedan parked on the
  street in front of the shop that night. It looked like the one
  Mr. Webb drives."
- Innocent alternative the board must show: the tow record or the
  neighbor's identification of the car could be mistaken; a sedan
  of the same model and color is not proof of the same car.
- Timeline collision: the car cannot have sat on the shop lift all
  week and also been delivered home Friday and seen at the shop
  Sunday night. The 9 PM hour on the Clock shows the collision.

**C2 — the bookkeeper's clock (MEDIUM, statement vs statement).**
- A (doc05, Whitfield, 2019): "I left the shop Friday at five
  o'clock and I did not come back until Monday morning."
  Repeated in doc06 (2020): "I wasn't at the shop that weekend
  at all."
- B (doc07, Whitfield, 2021): "I did stop by the shop that Sunday
  afternoon, briefly, to drop off invoices."
- Innocent alternative: a twenty minute errand two years later is
  exactly the kind of thing memory drops; she volunteered it when
  asked a new question.
