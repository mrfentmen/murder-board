The Duck File, a real case

Run blind, twice. The defense lawyer's unknown person claim caught at HIGH against his own filing, Offerd CONTRADICTED, and the board never claimed the masked men on camera were anybody. The brief rebuilt the half hour: 3:59, about 4:02, 4:26.

Miss, both runs: Turpin's unnamed friend is in neither gap list. Famous case: it lives in the models' training data, and recall alone cannot score here, every finding still needed a quote.

The baseline it had to beat

One single Argon call per case, the whole file, one plain instruction. It found the seams too, credit where due. Then the discipline failed: Whitfield overcalled CONTRADICTED, seven HIGHs on one file including a motive dressed as a contradiction, lead scores of 98, 92, 88 with no arithmetic, and a top lead that read re-interview and arrest, language this board is built never to use. The squad's edge is not finding. It is the quote check with its drop count in the open, severity that discriminates, scores the runner recomputes, and no person ever judged.

Limits, stated

Three of the four cases were written by this team, which is why this project declares Problem Statement One and not Two. The baseline is one run per case, not a distribution. The Decisions API does not exist in the event key's model list, checked live; the decision function here is the rubric recompute in the runner, deterministic, its arithmetic shown on every lead.
