SCENE REPORT — Delgado Auto Body, 1140 Fifth Avenue
Reporting officer: T. Brennan, Patrol. Date: October 14, 2019.

At 0712 hours I responded to a call of a person down at Delgado Auto
Body. The caller, Dana Whitfield, the shop's bookkeeper, stated she
arrived for work at approximately 0705 hours and found the owner,
Raymond Delgado, on the floor of the shop office. She called 911 at
approximately 0710 hours. Mr. Delgado was pronounced at the scene.

The office is at the rear of the shop. The body was behind the desk.
Two shell casings were observed on the office floor near the desk.
No firearm was observed in the office or the shop bays.

The front door was unlocked when the caller arrived. There were no
signs of forced entry at the front door, the bay doors, or the office
window. The office desk was disturbed; one drawer stood open.

The diner across Fifth Avenue has a camera facing the shop front.
Footage not yet requested.

The scene was held for detectives and the medical examiner. The
caller's statement was taken separately.
