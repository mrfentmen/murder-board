MEDICAL EXAMINER SUMMARY — Delgado, Raymond, 58
Office of the Medical Examiner. Examination date: October 15, 2019.

Decedent: Raymond "Ray" Delgado, male, 58 years, owner of Delgado
Auto Body, found in his shop office on the morning of October 14,
2019.

Cause of death: two gunshot wounds to the chest.
Manner of death: homicide.

Based on body temperature, livor, and rigor at the time of the scene
examination, death occurred between 9:00 PM and midnight on Sunday,
October 13, 2019.

No defensive wounds were observed. Toxicology was negative for
alcohol and common sedatives. The decedent's clothing and effects
were released to the investigating unit. Full autopsy report follows
under separate cover.
