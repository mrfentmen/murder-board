STATEMENT OF MARCUS WEBB — taken October 15, 2019
Interviewing detective: Det. R. Osei. Location: precinct interview room.

Q: State your relationship to Raymond Delgado.
A: Partners. Nine years. We own Delgado Auto Body together, fifty
fifty. Ray ran the floor, I ran the money side with Dana.

Q: When did you last see Mr. Delgado?
A: Friday afternoon at the shop. He was fine. We talked about a
transmission job coming in Monday.

Q: Where were you Sunday night, October 13?
A: Home. I was home alone Sunday night. I never left the house. I
watched television and went to bed early.

Q: Did you drive anywhere that weekend?
A: I couldn't. My car was in the shop all that week. Transmission
was shot. It sat on the lift at the shop from Monday on.

Q: Do you own a firearm?
A: No. I never have.

Q: Is there anything else we should know?
A: Ray owed money around. Suppliers, mostly. It wasn't my side of
the business.
