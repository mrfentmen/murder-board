STATEMENT OF MARCUS WEBB — follow-up, taken February 12, 2020
Interviewing detective: Det. R. Osei.

Q: We want to go over the weekend of October 12 and 13 again.
A: I've told you all of it. I was home that Sunday night, alone,
the whole night. Nothing has changed.

Q: And your car?
A: Like I said in October, the car was at the shop. It was up on
the lift with the transmission out. I didn't have wheels that
weekend, which is part of why I stayed in.

Q: Under your partnership agreement, what happens to the shop if
one partner dies?
A: The survivor takes full ownership. That's standard. It was
Ray's idea as much as mine, years ago.

Q: Did Mr. Delgado owe you money personally?
A: The shop owed suppliers. Ray and I were square.
