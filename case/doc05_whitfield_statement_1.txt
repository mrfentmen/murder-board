STATEMENT OF DANA WHITFIELD — taken October 14, 2019
Interviewing detective: Det. R. Osei.

Q: Tell us about this morning.
A: I got to the shop at five past seven, like always. The front
door was unlocked, which was wrong. I found Ray in the office, on
the floor behind his desk. I called 911 right away, about ten past
seven.

Q: Do you have a key to the shop?
A: Yes. I do the books, I open up most mornings. Ray had one,
Marcus had one, and I had one.

Q: When were you last at the shop before this morning?
A: I left the shop Friday at five o'clock and I did not come back
until Monday morning. I was home all weekend.

Q: Did Mr. Delgado seem worried about anything?
A: He was behind with two suppliers. He said he'd settle it this
month. That's all I know.
