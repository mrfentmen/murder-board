STATEMENT OF DANA WHITFIELD — taken June 9, 2020
Interviewing detective: Det. R. Osei. Topic: shop accounts.

Q: We are reviewing the shop's books for 2019. Who owed the shop
money that fall?
A: A few fleet accounts were late. The big one was internal, the
shop owed the parts suppliers, not the other way around.

Q: Were you at the shop at any time over the weekend of October 12
and 13, 2019?
A: No. I wasn't at the shop that weekend at all. Like I said last
year, I left Friday and came back Monday and found him.

Q: Who else had keys?
A: Ray, Marcus, and me. Eddie Kowalski had the bay code for
after-hours drop-offs, not a key.

Q: Did Mr. Webb take money out of the shop that fall?
A: Not that I ever saw in the books.
