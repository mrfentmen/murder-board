STATEMENT OF DANA WHITFIELD — taken March 3, 2021
Interviewing detective: Det. S. Park. Topic: cold case review.

Q: Is there anything about that weekend you haven't told us?
A: There's one thing I never mentioned because it didn't seem
important. I did stop by the shop that Sunday afternoon, briefly,
to drop off invoices. I had my key. I was there maybe twenty
minutes, in the front office, not Ray's office. I didn't see
anyone and nothing looked wrong.

Q: Why didn't you mention this in 2019 or 2020?
A: Nobody asked me about Sunday afternoon directly. And after I
found him Monday, I just kept thinking about Monday. It was twenty
minutes. The invoices were on my desk.

Q: What time Sunday?
A: Mid afternoon. Three, maybe four o'clock.
