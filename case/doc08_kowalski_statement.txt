STATEMENT OF EDWARD "EDDIE" KOWALSKI — taken October 16, 2019
Interviewing detective: Det. R. Osei.

Q: You run tows for Delgado Auto Body?
A: Kowalski Towing. I've done their tows maybe six years. Pickups,
drop-offs, after-hours cars.

Q: Did you do any work for the shop the week before Mr. Delgado
died?
A: One job. I towed Mr. Webb's blue sedan out of the shop on
Friday, October 11, and took it to his house. He said he needed
it for the weekend.

Q: The car was being repaired, wasn't it?
A: Transmission was out of it, or going in, I don't do the
mechanical side. We pushed it onto the flatbed. It wasn't
driveable, but he wanted it home.

Q: What time was that?
A: Late afternoon Friday. The yard log has the exact time.

Q: Were you at the shop Sunday?
A: No. No calls that Sunday at all.
