STATEMENT OF ROSA DELGADO — taken October 17, 2019
Interviewing detective: Det. R. Osei. Relationship: sister of the
decedent.

Q: When did you last speak with your brother?
A: Sunday evening. He called me at 6:40, we talked maybe ten
minutes. He sounded like himself. He said he was finishing
paperwork at the shop and going home after.

Q: Did he mention meeting anyone?
A: No. He said the shop was quiet. He was going to lock up and
get dinner.

Q: Did your brother owe money, or fear anyone?
A: He owed the parts people, like always. He wasn't afraid of
anyone. He and Marcus argued about money sometimes, but they
argued for nine years.

Q: Who should we talk to?
A: Everyone at that shop. Somebody knows the last person who saw
him, and it wasn't me.
