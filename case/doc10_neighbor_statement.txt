STATEMENT OF ELLEN VASQUEZ — taken March 18, 2021
Taken by: Det. S. Park, cold case review. Witness came forward
after a news segment on unsolved cases. She lives across Fifth
Avenue from Delgado Auto Body.

Q: What do you remember about the night of Sunday, October 13,
2019?
A: I heard what sounded like a car backfiring, twice, around
eleven thirty that night. I remember because it woke the dog and
I looked out the window.

Q: What did you see?
A: The shop was dark. There was a blue sedan parked on the street
in front of the shop that night. It looked like the one Mr. Webb
drives. I can't swear it was his, but it was the same kind of car.

Q: Why didn't you come forward in 2019?
A: Nobody knocked on my door. I didn't connect a backfire to
anything until I saw the news piece.
