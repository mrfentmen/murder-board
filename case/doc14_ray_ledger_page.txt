RAY'S PAPER LEDGER — final page (photograph, transcribed)

Sept 30 — Harbor Parts, owe 6,300. Pay Oct.
Oct 2 — Fleet acct (City Livery) owes shop 2,850. Called twice.
Oct 5 — M.W. took 1,900 from register for "supplier run." No
receipt yet.
Oct 8 — Harbor Parts again. Told them the 15th.
Oct 10 — M.W. owes shop 14,200 all in. Settle by Nov or it comes
out of his share.
Oct 12 — M — buyout talk again Tues. He wants numbers I don't
have.

[Transcriber note: handwriting consistent with the rest of the
book. Page is the last written page; the remainder of the book is
blank.]
