Kings County News, October 13, 2025. By staff reports.

Rapper Lil Trey, 19, shot outside Nostrand Avenue studio

A 19 year old Brooklyn drill artist was shot late Sunday night
outside the recording studio where he had been working, police
said.

The victim, Treyvon Ellis, who performed as Lil Trey, was found
on the sidewalk outside 1140 Nostrand Avenue shortly after
residents reported gunfire. Police said the shooting occurred
between 11:00 p.m. and midnight on Sunday, October 12. Ellis was
taken to Kings County Hospital, where he was pronounced dead.

Residents on the block told reporters they heard several shots.
One resident said a gray sedan left the block at speed shortly
after the shots. No arrests have been made. Police asked anyone
with video from the block to come forward.

Ellis had been scheduled to perform Friday at a showcase in
Flatbush. His management said the show would become a memorial.
