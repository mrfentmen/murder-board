Scene report, Officer D. Whitaker, October 10, 2016, 7:40 a.m.

At 6:55 a.m. I responded to the Harborlight Restaurant, 88 Pier
Road, on a report of an unresponsive employee. The day manager
let me in through the kitchen entrance.

The victim, Elena Marsh, 41, the restaurant's night manager,
was on the floor of the manager's office. A heavy brass desk
lamp lay on the floor beside her.

The office door latch is defective. The door appears closed
from the hallway but does not catch unless it is pulled hard.
The office window blinds were closed. The office door has a
keypad lock; the keypad keeps an entry log.

No signs of forced entry anywhere in the building. The kitchen
and dining room were undisturbed. The register was closed out.
The victim's handbag was on the office desk, undisturbed.

The night watchman, Tomas Herrera, was on scene. He stated he
found nothing unusual on his rounds during the night.
