Statement of Tomas Herrera, 52, night watchman, Harborlight
Marina and Restaurant. Taken October 10, 2016.

I have worked nights at the marina for nine years. I do my
rounds on the hour. Sunday night I did the 11 o'clock round
and the half past midnight round. Everything was quiet.

I never entered the office that night. I do not go in the
office on my rounds. I check the door from the hallway.

At the 11 o'clock round the office was dark. The manager
usually leaves by 11. I saw nobody in the parking lot after
10.

I heard nothing during the night. If there had been a fight I
would have heard it. I was sorry to hear about Elena. She was
strict but fair.
