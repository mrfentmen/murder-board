Statement of Priya Raman, 29, relief manager, Harborlight
Restaurant. Taken October 11, 2016.

I want to say this first, before anyone asks: I went back to
the office that night. I left my bag in the office at the end
of my shift. I came back at about ten to eleven and used my
own code to get in. Elena was there, alive, doing the close
out. We talked for maybe two minutes. I took my bag and I
left. I was home by 11:30 and asleep by 12.

I did not lend my code to anybody. We are not supposed to
share codes, and I never have.

Elena got the manager job over me last spring. People will
say that matters. It did not, not like this.
