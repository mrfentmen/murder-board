Second statement of Marcus Delaney. Taken March 3, 2017,
after the toll record was shown to him.

Fine. I drove to the marina that night. I got there at about
five past 11. I wanted to talk to Elena about the custody
papers before the hearing. I sat in the lot for maybe fifteen
minutes. The office light was off, so I figured she had
already left, and I drove home.

I never got out of the truck. I never went inside. I lied the
first time because my lawyer said anything about that night
would be used in the custody case. I am sorry I lied. I did
not kill her.
