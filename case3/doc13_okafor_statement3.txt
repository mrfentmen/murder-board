Third statement of Daniel Okafor. Taken June 17, 2024, in a
cold case review.

I have had eight years to think about that night. I left
around 10, maybe a little after. I did stop at the supply
closet on my way out, and Elena came out of the office and
we talked for a minute about the Tuesday delivery. I do not
remember if I said that before. Then I went home.

I have told the truth every time. Memories get fuzzy, that
is all. I did not go back to that restaurant that night.
