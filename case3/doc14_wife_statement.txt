Statement of Grace Okafor, wife of Daniel Okafor. Taken
June 17, 2024, in a cold case review.

Daniel came home that night before 11. I remember because
the late news was not on yet. He was home all night. He was
asleep by about 12, next to me.

I was never asked about this in 2016. Nobody called me. I am
saying it now because it is true.
