Harbor Gazette, October 13, 2016. "Marina manager's death
shocks the pier."

Police are investigating the death of Elena Marsh, 41, found
in her office at the Harborlight Restaurant early Monday.

A source close to the investigation noted that the office
door's latch was known to be faulty, and that the door may
not have been locked at all that night.

Police are looking closely at the victim's former husband,
who was seen in the area that night, the Gazette has learned.
No arrests have been made.
