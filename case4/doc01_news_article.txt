News report, August 5, 2020. Summarized from Chicago Tribune
and Chicago Sun-Times reporting of August 4 and 5, 2020.

Rapper FBG Duck killed in Gold Coast shooting

Chicago rapper FBG Duck was shot and killed Tuesday afternoon
on East Oak Street in the Gold Coast, one of the city's most
upscale shopping districts, police said.

The rapper, whose real name was Carlton Weekly, was 26. He had
been shopping for a gift for his son. At approximately 4:26
p.m., two vehicles pulled up near the 100 block of East Oak
Street. Four gunmen got out, two from each vehicle, and opened
fire. Weekly was shot multiple times. Two other people were
wounded, a woman who was with Weekly and a man nearby.

Weekly was taken to Northwestern Memorial Hospital, where he
was pronounced dead about half an hour later.

The gunmen got back into the two vehicles and fled. Police said
the shooting was targeted. No one was in custody Tuesday night.
Detectives said they were reviewing surveillance video from the
block, which is lined with luxury stores and cameras.

Weekly was a well known figure in Chicago drill music. Police
said he was involved in an ongoing gang conflict.
