Chicago Police Department statement at the scene, August 4,
2020. Deputy Chief Daniel O'Shea, summarized from news
reporting of the police briefing.

Deputy Chief O'Shea said the shoppers were standing on the
sidewalk when a pair of vehicles pulled up, with two gunmen
stepping out of each to open fire.

He said the shooting was a targeted attack, not a random one.
One weapon was recovered by police at the scene.

O'Shea said Weekly had been involved in an ongoing conflict
with another gang. Officers noted that Weekly had been
broadcasting his travels on social media that day, and that he
was found.

Police said the two vehicles fled the scene and that
detectives were reviewing security footage from the area.
Police asked anyone with video from the block to come forward.
