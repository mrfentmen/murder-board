Surveillance video summary. From the video Chicago police
released in August 2020 and the camera evidence described at
trial.

Surveillance video from the Parkway Gardens apartments, the
South Side complex known as O Block, shows four men sprinting
down the steps of a building and getting into two waiting cars:
a Ford Fusion and a Chrysler 300.

Police POD cameras and license plate readers then tracked the
two cars as they drove from the South Side to the Gold Coast.

Video from Oak Street shows the Ford Fusion and the Chrysler
300 pull up. Four gunmen got out of the two cars and shot
Weekly and the two people with him. The gunmen at Oak Street
were masked.

Police POD cameras tracked the two vehicles as they drove back
toward Parkway Gardens after the shooting. The two cars then
split up in different directions.

Police released the Parkway Gardens video and asked the public
to help identify the four men.
