Federal detention hearing summary, October 15, 2021.
Assistant U.S. Attorney Jason Julien, as reported by the
Chicago Sun-Times.

Prosecutors said defendant Tacarlos Offerd purchased a vehicle
used as a "lead car" in the shooting one week before the
August 4, 2020 murder. The vehicle was a Ford Fusion.

Julien said the Fusion was returned to the dealership in the
Chicago suburbs approximately one hour after the shooting.

He said the vehicle Offerd purchased was seen on police POD
cameras and license plate readers.

Offerd's defense attorney, John Somerville, argued there was
no evidence linking anyone specific to driving the car that
day. "Mr. Offerd bought a car," Somerville said. "Some unknown
person was in his car. Some unknown person may have driven
downtown."

Prosecutors said Offerd drove the Fusion in the shooting, with
gunmen riding in it.
