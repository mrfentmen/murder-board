Summary of the government's Santiago proffer, federal court
filing in United States v. Liggins, filed August 4, 2023.

The second car in the shooting was a Chrysler 300. Prosecutors
said Kenneth Roberson drove the Chrysler 300, with gunmen
riding in it.

CPD seized Roberson's Chrysler 300 the day after the murder.

After the seizure, Roberson called CPD to get it back.

The Chrysler appears with the Ford Fusion in the surveillance
trail: out of Parkway Gardens before the shooting, on Oak
Street at the shooting, and tracked back toward the South Side
after it.
