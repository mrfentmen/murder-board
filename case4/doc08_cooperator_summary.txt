Summary of the government's Santiago proffer, federal court
filing in United States v. Liggins, filed August 4, 2023. The
filing describes statements Kenneth Roberson made to people
who later cooperated with the government.

As the crew drove toward East Oak Street on August 4, 2020,
Roberson made statements to a cooperating witness acknowledging
that Roberson was on his way to murder Weekly.

After the murder, Roberson made additional statements about it
to the same cooperating witness.

Roberson later made statements about the murder to a second
witness. The filing says those statements were made for
purposes of concealment.

The filing does not name the cooperating witnesses. It refers
to them by number.
