Trial evidence summary: the social media and music evidence.
From the federal trial, October 2023 to January 2024, and the
U.S. Attorney's statements.

The U.S. Attorney's Office said the O Block gang "publicly
claimed responsibility for their violence and used social
media and music to increase their criminal enterprise."

Prosecutors built the racketeering case in part from the
defendants' own posts, photos, and videos: gang signs, firearms,
and the feud with Tookaville carried on in public, in songs and
online, over years.

Jurors saw video recorded after the murder in which King Von,
the O Block rapper prosecutors said put a price on Weekly's
head, gave the defendants diamond O Block pendants and stacks
of cash.

Prosecutors told the jury the defendants believed killing FBG
Duck would bring glory to O Block: it was about power, status,
and retribution.
