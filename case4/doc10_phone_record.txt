Phone record summary. From the government's Santiago
proffer, federal court filing in United States v. Liggins,
filed August 4, 2023.

Ralph Turpin placed at least one phone call from inside Milani
Boutique on the afternoon of August 4, 2020. The filing says
the call reported Weekly's location to his rivals.

The call came after Turpin entered the boutique at
approximately 3:59 p.m. and saw Weekly inside.

At about 4:02 p.m., roughly three minutes after Turpin entered
the boutique, the O Block crew began moving toward East Oak
Street.

The shooting was at approximately 4:26 p.m. The call, the
drive, and the shooting sit inside one half hour.
