U.S. Department of Justice summary of the indictments.
Original indictment October 2021; superseding indictment
unsealed April 10, 2023.

Five Chicago men were charged in October 2021 with committing
murder in aid of racketeering and conspiracy to commit murder
in aid of racketeering: Charles Liggins, also known as "C
Murda"; Kenneth Roberson, also known as "Kenny Mac"; Tacarlos
Offerd, also known as "Los"; Christopher Thomas, also known as
"C Thang"; and Marcus Smart, also known as "Muwop."

In April 2023 a superseding indictment added a sixth
defendant: Ralph Turpin, also known as "Tall" and "Teezy,"
charged with murder in aid of racketeering and conspiracy to
commit murder in aid of racketeering.

The indictment alleged the six were members or associates of
the O Block street gang, and that they murdered Carlton Weekly
to maintain and increase their positions in the gang.

News reporting identified a fourth gunman, Ezell Rawls, in the
surveillance video. Rawls died during the investigation and
was never tried.
