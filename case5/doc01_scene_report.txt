SCENE REPORT - Starlite Lanes, 88 Meridian Avenue. Reporting officer: P. Tate, Patrol. Date: November 5, 2017.

At 0605 hours I responded to a call from the morning opener, who found the owner, Carla Ibanez, 47, on the floor of the office behind the counter. She was pronounced at the scene at 0622 hours.

The office door was closed and the latch engaged. There were no signs of forced entry at the office door or the back door. The victim was behind the desk. On the desk was a league scoresheet dated November 4.

The cover of the lane 12 ball return stood open. The rest of the lanes were dark. The keypad panel beside the office door showed a green ready light.
