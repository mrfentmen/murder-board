STATEMENT OF TOMAS "TOMMY" REYES - taken November 5, 2017, Det. R. Alvarez.

I am the night mechanic at Starlite Lanes. I have worked there six years. I fix the pinsetters and the ball returns.

Saturday I came in at six to work on lane 7. I finished the belt job and I left at eleven and never went back. Carla was in the office doing the books when I left. She was fine.

I drove home. I passed the building again around twelve thirty on my way to get cigarettes and the building was dark, lanes off, office dark. Nobody was there.

I would never hurt Carla. She cut my weekday hours in October, sure, but that is business.
