STATEMENT OF MARCUS BELL - taken November 6, 2017, Det. R. Alvarez.

I captain the Saturday league at Starlite. I left my ball in the locker room Saturday night, so I went back for it. I let myself in at 11:20, on my own code. Carla was in the office. We talked for a minute about the league banquet. She was alive and fine and working on the scoresheets.

I took my ball and left. I did not see anybody else in the building. I am telling you this myself because my code is on that log and I would rather you heard it from me.
