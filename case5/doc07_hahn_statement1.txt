STATEMENT OF DEREK HAHN - taken November 7, 2017, Det. R. Alvarez.

I worked the counter at Starlite until Carla fired me on October 12. She said I was shorting the register. I was not.

Saturday night I was home all night. I did not go near the lanes. I have no reason to go back there and I did not.

I heard she was dead on the news like everybody else.
