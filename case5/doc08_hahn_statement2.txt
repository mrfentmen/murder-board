STATEMENT OF DEREK HAHN - taken March 14, 2019, Det. R. Alvarez.

Fine. I drove there that night. I wanted my last paycheck and I wanted to say it to her face. I got to the lot around eleven ten. I knocked on the office door off the back hall. Nobody answered. The office window was dark from where I stood.

I left before eleven forty. I never went inside. I lied in 2017 because I knew how it would look, a fired man at the door that night. I am not lying now. She did not answer, and I left.
