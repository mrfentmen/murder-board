STATEMENT OF JOSEFINA LUNA - taken November 5, 2017, Det. R. Alvarez.

I clean Starlite Lanes at night. Saturday I started at nine thirty. I did the bathrooms, the concourse, and the back hall.

I finished and left at 11:45. The lanes were quiet. Carla was in the office. Mr. Reyes had already gone, I thought. I locked the side door behind me and went home.

I saw nobody in the back hall while I cleaned.
