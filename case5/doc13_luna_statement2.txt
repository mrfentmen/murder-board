STATEMENT OF JOSEFINA LUNA - taken June 9, 2025, cold case review.

I have thought about that night for years. I left around midnight, I would say now. While I mopped the back hall I remember the machines running on lane 12, the pins resetting. I did not put that in my first statement because nobody asked me about sounds, and at the time I did not think it mattered.

In 2017 I said 11:45 because that is when I usually finish. In 2025 I am telling you what I actually remember: the lane 12 machine was running while I was still there.
