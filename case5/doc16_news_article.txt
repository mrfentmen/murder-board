NEWS ARTICLE - Meridian Ledger, October 18, 2016. "Break-in at Starlite Lanes."

Thieves entered Starlite Lanes through the rear service door early Sunday, taking the league prize safe. Owner Carla Ibanez said the door's latch had been faulty for months and that she had reported it twice.

Police said there were no suspects. Ibanez said the safe held about four thousand dollars in league prize money.
