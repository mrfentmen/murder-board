EVIDENCE MEMO - Det. R. Alvarez, November 20, 2017.

League member Diane Pruitt told me she took a photograph from the parking lot at 12:55 AM on November 5, showing the office window of Starlite Lanes lit, with the blinds partly open. She showed me the photograph on her phone during our conversation on November 12.

The original photograph was never collected. Ms. Pruitt has since replaced the phone. A request to her carrier for the cloud backup is outstanding in the file; no response is recorded.

The photograph is referenced in two later statements but the image itself is not in the evidence ledger.
